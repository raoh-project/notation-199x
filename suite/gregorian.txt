# The days of the proleptic Gregorian calendar, counted from 1970-01-01.
#
# Source:
#   Souther specification.adoc — stdlib-date, primitives (the calendar is the proleptic ISO one)
#   README.md — the domain of the entry points that take a year, a month and a day
#
# year ; month ; day ; outcome ; epoch day
#
# year, month and day are signed decimals, the year astronomical: the year before 1 is 0. outcome is
# DAY, where the three name a day of a year from -999999999 to 999999999, or NONE; epoch day is the
# day's count from 1970-01-01, negative before it, and is empty where the outcome is NONE.
#
# A year is a leap year when 4 divides it and 100 does not, or when 400 does: 2000, 0 and -400 are,
# 1900, 2100 and -100 are not. The months are the lines 2023 gives, each at its last day and one
# past it.
